PRODUCT CONCERNED
Product     : Levothyroxine Sodium Tablets
Strength    : 50 mcg
Dosage form : Tablet
Batch No    : LEV2604A
Mfg date    : 11 Feb 2025
Expiry      : Jan 2027
Quantity    : 14 strips from a single shipper carton

DESCRIPTION OF DEFECT
During a routine dispensary stock check our technician identified that the
outer carton declares a strength of 50 mcg while the blister foil overprint on
fourteen strips reads 25 mcg. The tablet debossing could not be used to confirm
the true strength visually.

No units from the affected strips have been dispensed to patients. The stock has
been isolated in a quarantine bin within the dispensary and is available for
collection.

We consider this a potentially serious labelling discrepancy given that
levothyroxine is a narrow therapeutic index product and request an urgent
assessment of whether other batches are affected.

Sample available for collection: Yes

Dr. Helen Marsh
