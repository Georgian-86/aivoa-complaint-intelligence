We are writing to raise a formal market complaint on behalf of Meridian Pharma
Distribution, Hyderabad.

On 12 September 2026, our warehouse QA team performed a routine visual
inspection of a returned consignment of Ceftrioxam 1 g Injection (Lyophilised
Powder for Injection). During reconstitution checks on retained units, black
fibrous particulate matter was clearly visible in the reconstituted solution of
multiple vials.

Product details as printed on the carton:

  Product Name    : Ceftrioxam 1 g Injection
  Strength        : 1 g
  Batch No.       : CFX24B902
  Mfg. Date       : 03/2025
  Exp. Date       : 02/2027
  Pack            : 10 vials per carton

Quantity affected: 38 vials across 4 cartons from the same shipper.

Two hospitals in our distribution network have already reported that a patient
receiving this product developed a febrile reaction and required overnight
hospitalisation for observation. The treating physician has raised this with us
directly. We have immediately quarantined all remaining stock of this batch at
our depot (a further 620 vials).

The affected vials have been segregated and are available for collection by
your courier for investigation. Please confirm the collection arrangement and
advise whether the remaining batch quantity should be returned.

We request your urgent response given the patient safety implications.
